Ātman

आत्मन्
BG 2.55 · 13.31–32 · 14.19–20  ·  Direct Gita Teaching
The Witness Consciousness

Pure consciousness, the eternal Witness. One who sees the guṇas as the sole active agents in nature, and knows the Self as transcendental to them, attains the Divine nature.

Core PrincipleThe observed changes. The witness remains untouched.
PracticeNotice the quiet awareness that is aware of thoughts, moods, and desires — that noticing was never sattvic, rajasic, or tamasic.
